# Technical Documentation: Role 3 (Stage 2) — Dead-Ball (`D`) Segmentation & Frame-Zero Restart Detection

**Competition:** More than a Hack 2027 — Phase 1: Frame-Level Ball Possession (FC Barcelona vs Athletic Club)  
**Module Scope:** Role 3 / Stage 2 (`src/deadball/`) — Spatio-Temporal Dead-Ball Segmentation, Restart Team Estimation, and Exact Boundary Snapping  
**Output Contract:** `data/interim/stage2_deadball.parquet` (`199,319` frames, 25.0 fps, columns: `frame_id`, `p_dead`, `is_set_piece_window`, `restart_team_hint`, zero `NaN` values)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Role in the Pipeline

In the *More than a Hack 2027* challenge, submissions are ranked primarily on **`spellF1`** (one-to-one bipartite temporal IoU matching of maximal possession runs over thresholds $\tau \in \{0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7\}$), with **`macroF1`** serving as the tie-breaker. Within our team's multi-stage architecture—where **Role 4** resolves live team possession (`H` vs `A`) using a model trained on external match datasets—**Role 3 (Stage 2)** serves as the structural backbone responsible for isolating out-of-play stoppages (`D`) and pinpointing the exact frame play resumes ("Frame 0"):

1. **Protecting Possession Continuity & Separation (`spellF1`):** A false-positive `D` spike in the middle of live play splits a single true possession into two shorter spells, generating **one False Negative and two False Positives** under temporal IoU matching. Conversely, failing to detect a genuine quick throw-in or free kick ($\ge 1.0\text{ s}$) merges two distinct possessions into one.
2. **Sub-Second Boundary Precision ($\pm 1\text{ s}$ Penalty):** As shown in `EVALUATION.md`, a 1-second (25-frame) boundary offset degrades `spellF1` from `1.0000` to `0.6539`. Because automated Smart Tagging exhibits a $\pm 2\text{--}5\text{ s}$ temporal jitter and player kinematics lag behind ball out-of-bounds events due to physical inertia, naive smoothing fails at the margins. Furthermore, any post-restart dead-ball "tail" delays Role 4's takeover of the new live possession.
3. **Surviving 41% Missing Ball Frames:** The ball is absent on **40.7% of graded frames** (`94,214 / 158,857` visible). During set-piece setups and aerial restarts, the tactical detector frequently loses the ball (`ball_status_code > 0`).

To solve these challenges, Role 3 implements a deterministic, fully vectorized three-module architecture (`smart_tags.py`, `kinematic_d.py`, `d_model.py`) that fuses multi-camera tracking (Tactical + synchronized Broadcast from Stage 0/1), automated tactical priors, FIFA geometric exclusion laws, ultra-sharp dual-edge impulse snapping (with a 3-frame post-restart decay), and a responsive 2-state Viterbi decoder in log-odds space.

While Role 4 utilizes external match data to train its live possession classifier, Role 3 relies strictly on biomechanical constraints, Laws of the Game geometry, and multi-camera signal processing—using zero manual annotations, hand-corrected frames, or external event feeds from FC Barcelona vs Athletic Club.

## 4. Detailed Module Architecture & Implemented Rules

### 4.1. Module 1: Temporal Priors & `Team = N/A` Resolution (`src/deadball/smart_tags.py`)

1. **Frame Alignment & Extended Core Plateau:** XML `<start>` and `<end>` timestamps (in seconds) are mapped to tactical video frames `[0..199318]` via $\text{frame} = \text{clip}(\text{round}(t_s \times 25), 0, 199318)$. To prevent premature truncation of long set-piece preparations, we construct a trapezoidal prior `sp_prior_prob` maintaining a high plateau of $0.90$ across the entire core tag $[f_{\text{start}} - 30, f_{\text{end}} - 5]$, surrounded by linear ramps over $\pm 3.5\text{ s}$ ($\pm 88$ frames, from $0.20$ up to $0.90$ and down to $0.12$).
2. **TV Replay Anchors (`brd_is_replay`):** When the broadcast director cuts away from the wide pitch view during active play (`brd_is_replay == 1`, `period > 0`), the game is stopped. We project a dead-ball anchor shifted $40$ frames backward (accounting for the director's reaction time after the whistle) and cut the final $30$ frames of the replay core ($0.90$ in the safe core, $0.65$ in the margins) because TV feeds frequently return $1\text{--}2\text{ s}$ after the referee's restart whistle.
3. **Live-Play Suppression & Onset Signals:** Active offensive/transition tagsOutside set-piece windows generate a negative prior `live_play_suppression` ($0.30$ for `ATTACKING TRANSITION`, `CREATING CHANCES`, `PROGRESSION`; $0.16$ for `BUILD UP`, `BALL IN FINAL THIRD`, `BALL IN THE BOX`) that prevents slow build-up phases from triggering false stoppages. Simultaneously, the start frame of `BUILD UP`, `LONG BALL`, `PROGRESSION`, and `BALL IN THE BOX` tags generates a triangular `live_tag_onset_signal` ($\pm 12$ frames, peak $0.65$) that aids "Frame 0" detection.
4. **Multi-Cue Resolution of `Team = N/A` (All 26 Throw-ins):** In the provided XML, 36 instances—including **100% of `Throw-in` tags (26/26)**—carry `Team = N/A`. Our solver `resolve_na_restart_team()` deterministically infers the restarting team (`H` vs `A`) by combining four vectorized cues across $[f_{\text{start}} + 0.35(f_{\text{end}} - f_{\text{start}}),\; f_{\text{end}} + 45]$:
   * **Boundary Taker Geometry:** Weighting frames (`3.5` weight) where `ball_near_boundary == 1` and one team has a player within $< 2.2\text{ m}$ of the ball while the opponent is $> 1.2\text{ m}$ further away (`away_near1_dist_m - home_near1_dist_m`).
   * **Exclusive 3m Control Zone:** Comparing the proportion of frames where exclusively one team occupies the $3\text{ m}$ control radius (`home_in_3m >= 1 & away_in_3m == 0` vs vice versa).
   * **Post-Restart Pitch Control Jump:** Integrating `pitch_ctrl_leadlag_25f` across $[f_{\text{end}} - 25, f_{\text{end}} + 35]$.
   * **Subsequent Tactical Tag Votes:** Summing team ownership votes from overlapping/subsequent live tags (with a $2.5\times$ boost on tag onsets), automatically inverting polarity for defensive tags (`DEFENDING IN ...` tagged for Team $X$ votes for possession by Team $\neg X$).

### 4.2. Module 2: Vectorized Kinematic & Geometric Detector (`src/deadball/kinematic_d.py`)

This module supports both raw 46-slot ATD data (aligning 1-indexed `frame_id` `1..199319` to `0..199318` and scaling coordinates to $[-52.5, 52.5]\text{ m} \times [-34.0, 34.0]\text{ m}$) and the enriched `stage1_tracking.parquet` schema:

1. **Collective Player Speed (`mean_speed_all`) & Walking Fraction (`walking_ratio_2ms`):** Evaluates mean velocity across all visible players ($s_{\text{speed}} = \text{clip}((3.3 - \bar{v}) / 2.0, 0, 1)$) and the fraction of players moving at walking pace ($< 2.0\text{ m/s}$, $s_{\text{walk}} = \text{clip}((\text{walk}_{2\text{ms}} - 0.32) / 0.50, 0, 1)$).
2. **Inter-Team Separation / No Pressing (`no_pressing_dist_m`):** Computed either via chunked 3D tensor broadcasting `(chunk, N_H, N_A)` on raw slots or analytically from formation centroids and spatial variances:
   $$d_{\text{H-A}} = \sqrt{\Vert\boldsymbol{\mu}_H - \boldsymbol{\mu}_A\Vert_2^2 + \sigma_{H,x}^2 + \sigma_{A,x}^2 + \sigma_{H,y}^2 + \sigma_{A,y}^2}$$
3. **Multi-Camera Out-of-Bounds Detection (`ball_out_of_bounds`):** When the tactical ball is latent (`ball_status_code > 0`) and the broadcast homography is valid (`brd_valid == 1`), we substitute `brd_ball_x` and `brd_ball_y`. Coordinates are scaled from $[0, 105.3] \times [0, 68.0]$ to $[-52.5, 52.5] \times [-34.0, 34.0]$ to flag excursions beyond $[-52.35, 52.35] \times [-33.85, 33.85]$, or where `ball_near_boundary == 1` with `ball_speed < 2.2 m/s`, reinforced when the TV camera focal point (`brd_lookat_x/y`) parks near a touchline/goal line (`brd_lookat_speed_ms < 2.2`).
4. **Static Set-Piece Structures (`static_structure_flag`):**
   * *Free-Kick Wall (`free_kick_wall_flag`):* Stationary ball ($\ge 15$ frames), uncontested (`is_contested == 0`), walking ratio $\ge 0.65$, and $\ge 3$ defenders clustered in the $7.5\text{--}12.0\text{ m}$ ring.
   * *Corner / Box Cluster (`corner_box_cluster_flag`):* $\ge 6$ players inside the penalty box (`players_in_penalty_box >= 6`) with a stationary ball ($\ge 10$ frames) and walking ratio $\ge 0.60$.
   * *Kick-Off & Goal-Kick Setups:* Ball within $3.5\text{ m}$ of $(0, 0)$ with halves separated (`home_centroid_x_norm < 51.5` and `away_centroid_x_norm > 53.5`), or goalkeeper within $< 4.0\text{ m}$ of a stationary ball inside the goal area ($|x| \ge 45.0\text{ m}, |y| \le 11.0\text{ m}$).
5. **FIFA Opponent Exclusion Law (`opponent_exclusion_score`):** Distinguishes slow live build-up from set-piece preparation. Scores frames where the taker is within $< 3.2\text{ m}$ (`one_team_in3 == True`), the ball is slow/stationary (`ball_speed < 2.2 m/s`), and the nearest opponent respects a distance $> 3.2\text{--}8.4\text{ m}$.
6. **Possession State-Machine Void & Immediate Chain Protection (`control_void_score`):** Uses `frames_since_last_control` rising above $18$ frames alongside walking ratios $> 0.28$ to boost `D` probability. Conversely, to avoid suppressing the immediate onset of a foul or out-of-bounds event right after a touch, active possession suppression (`raw_score * 0.45`) is strictly confined to immediate multi-touch chains (`max_touches >= 2`, `frames_since_last_control <= 10` frames $= 0.4\text{ s}$, and `~ball_out_of_bounds`).
7. **Ball Death Flux ($\Phi_{L \to D}$, `ball_death_flux`):** Captures the exact onset frame of a stoppage via the rising edge of `ball_out_of_bounds | ball_near_boundary` convolved with an 11-frame Hanning window (`0.80 * oob_flux`), combined with sudden collective deceleration (`speed_drop / 1.4`) following a recent physical challenge (`is_contested == 1` within the preceding $35$ frames, `0.60 * whistle_flux`).
8. **Temporal Filtering:** The composite kinematic score is smoothed with a responsive 15-frame ($0.60\text{ s}$) boxcar kernel to preserve short stoppages while eliminating single-frame tracking jitter.

### 4.3. Module 3: Dual-Edge Snapping & Responsive Viterbi Decoding (`src/deadball/d_model.py`)

1. **Bayesian Prior-Kinematic Fusion:** Inside set-piece windows (`is_set_piece_window == True`), raw dead-ball probability is fused as $p_{\text{in}} = 0.48 \cdot p_{\text{prior}} + 0.52 \cdot s_{\text{kin}}$. Outside tagged windows, confident out-of-bounds events yield $\text{clip}(0.58 + 0.38 \cdot s_{\text{kin}}, 0, 0.96)$, while general play evaluates $\text{clip}((s_{\text{kin}} - 0.30) \cdot 0.95 - 0.75 \cdot s_{\text{live\_supp}}, 0, 0.78)$.
2. **Restart Impulse Signal ($\Phi_{D \to L}$, "Frame 0" Detector):**
   `_compute_restart_impulse_signal()` fuses six physical and tactical edge detectors to locate the exact restart touch:
   * **Reliability-Weighted Ball Kick Impulse:** Combines ball acceleration (`ball_acc / 35.0`), 2-frame speed jump (`ball_speed - roll(ball_speed, 2)`), and trajectory deflection (`ball_deflection_angle`), gated by prior ball stationarity (`prev_stat / 8.0`). When the tactical ball is in a tracking gap (`ball_status_code > 0`), the model substitutes velocity and acceleration derived from synchronized broadcast ball coordinates (`brd_ball_x/y`, reliability $0.85$).
   * **Set-Piece Taker Burst:** 3-frame acceleration and closing speed (`near1_closing_speed_ms`) of the nearest player belonging to `restart_team_hint` within $3.2\text{ m}$ of the ball.
   * **State-Machine Contact Reset:** Detects the exact frame where `frames_since_last_control` drops from $\ge 22$ frames back to $\le 1$.
   * **Broadcast & Tactical Camera Whip-Pan:** Detects sudden camera acceleration (`brd_lookat_speed_ms` and `camera_pan_speed`) on long restarts (masked for the first $5$ frames after a broadcast replay cut using `frames_since_brd_replay > 5`).
   * **Lead/Lag Edge Filters & Tag Onsets:** Incorporates `|near1_diff_leadlag_10f|`, `|pitch_ctrl_leadlag_10f|`, and `live_tag_onset_signal`.
3. **Dual-Edge Boundary Snapping with Ultra-Fast Post-Restart Handover (`_snap_both_deadball_edges`):**
   * **Right Edge (`frame_zero`):** Searched across the final $55\%$ of each window (`s_idx + 0.45 * win_len` to `e_idx`), weighted by geometric compatibility `_compute_type_geometry_compatibility()` (sideline for `throw_in`, corner quadrant for `corner_kick`, goal area for `goal_kick`, center circle for `kick_off`). When `max(restart_score) > 0.34`, `frame_zero` snaps to the argmax frame $t_0$.
   * **3-Frame Post-Restart Decay:** Immediately at `frame_zero`, `p_dead` is crushed over a 3-frame window (`frame_zero : frame_zero + 3`, decay curve $0.05 \to 0.15 \times s_{\text{kin}}$). This eliminates dead-ball "tails" after the kick, allowing Role 4's live possession classifier to take over on the exact frame the ball enters play.
   * **Left Edge (`d_start`) with Gentle Pre-Whistle Tapering:** Snaps the onset of `D` to the local maximum of `ball_death_flux` ($\Phi_{L \to D}$). Prior to `d_start`, `p_refined` is gently tapered via `0.85 * kinematic_d_score` so genuine slow play leading into a stoppage is not artificially distorted.
4. **Global 2-State Viterbi Decoder & 1-Second Morphological Cleanup (`_viterbi_and_posterior_decode`):**
   We transform `p_dead_snapped` into log-odds $L_t = \log\frac{p_t}{1 - p_t}$ and run dynamic programming (Viterbi algorithm) separately over each half (`period in (1, 2)`). Transition penalties between `LIVE (0)` and `DEAD (1)` are modulated dynamically by our edge fluxes:
   $$C_{L \to D}(t) = \max\big(1.2,\; \lambda_{\text{base}} - 6.0 \cdot \Phi_{L \to D}(t)\big), \qquad C_{D \to L}(t) = \max\big(1.2,\; \lambda_{\text{base}} - 6.5 \cdot \Phi_{D \to L}(t)\big)$$
   with a responsive $\lambda_{\text{base}} = 8.5$. Following Viterbi decoding, a morphological filter bridges internal live gaps shorter than `min_live_gap_frames = 22` ($0.88\text{ s}$) and removes isolated dead-ball spikes shorter than `min_dead_duration_frames = 25` ($1.00\text{ s}$). This preserves quick throw-ins and fast free kicks ($\ge 1.0\text{ s}$) that genuinely separate consecutive possessions. Finally, `p_dead` is calibrated so that `p_dead >= 0.50` maps **1-to-1** to the Viterbi path ($[0.60, 0.99]$ for `DEAD`, $[0.01, 0.48]$ for `LIVE`), with hard domain overrides (`p_dead = 1.0`) for `period == 0`, `is_pre_kickoff_d == 1`, and `st_post_goal_dead == 1` ($0.99$).

## 5. Known Limitations & Future Improvements

1. **Sub-Second "Touch-and-Go" Free Kicks ($< 1.0\text{ s}$):** When a player is fouled in the middle third and a teammate immediately taps the rolling ball within $< 25$ frames ($1.0\text{ s}$) without the referee holding play or the TV director cutting to a replay, our morphological filter (`min_dead_duration_frames = 25`) may bridge the stoppage to avoid false-positive spell fragmentation.
2. **Contested Drop-Balls Without Set-Piece Tags:** Rare referee stoppages (e.g., head injury checks restarted via a drop-ball) lack a `SET PIECES` XML tag. While `brd_is_replay` and `opponent_exclusion_score` detect the stoppage, `frame_zero` localization lacks a strict boundary geometric multiplier (`throw_in`/`corner_kick`).
3. **Throw-In Fake Swings During Ball Occlusions:** If a throw-in taker performs a fake throw motion while the ball is occluded (`ball_status_code == 2` and `brd_valid == 0`), the spike in `taker_acc` can occasionally pull `frame_zero` $10\text{--}15$ frames ahead of the true release.

## 6. Output Schema & Reproduction Instructions

### 6.1. Output Schema (`data/interim/stage2_deadball.parquet`)

| Column | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `frame_id` | `int32` | Exact tactical video frame index `0 … 199318` in strictly ascending order. |
| `p_dead` | `float32` | Calibrated probability $[0.0, 1.0]$ that the ball is out of play (`D`). Threshold `p_dead >= 0.50` yields the exact Viterbi-optimal segmentation (`[0.60, 0.99]` for `DEAD`, `[0.01, 0.48]` for `LIVE`). Zero `NaN` values. |
| `is_set_piece_window` | `bool` | Active (`True`) inside expanded Smart Tagging set-piece anchors, post-goal intervals, and TV replay windows. |
| `restart_team_hint` | `category` (`'H'`, `'A'`, `NaN`) | Estimated team restarting play (`H` = FC Barcelona, `A` = Athletic Club), with all 26 `N/A` throw-ins and untagged stoppages resolved. |

### 6.2. How to Reproduce Using `uv`

From the root directory of the repository:

```bash

uv sync

# 2. Run the production Role 3 (Stage 2) Dead-Ball pipeline
uv run python -m src.deadball.d_model \
    --stage1 data/interim/stage1_tracking.parquet \
    --xml data/tactical/BAR-ATH_tactical_smart_tagging.xml \
    --output data/interim/stage2_deadball.parquet
```
