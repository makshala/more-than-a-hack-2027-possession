# Technical Documentation: Stage 2 — Dead-Ball (`D`) Segmentation & Frame-Zero Restart Detection

**Competition:** More than a Hack 2027 — Phase 1: Frame-Level Ball Possession (FC Barcelona vs Athletic Club)  
**Module Scope:** Stage 2 (`src/deadball/`) — Spatio-Temporal Dead-Ball Segmentation, Restart Team Estimation, and Exact Boundary Snapping  
**Output Contract:** `data/interim/stage2_deadball.parquet` (`199,319` frames, 25.0 fps, zero `NaN` values)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Motivation

In the *More than a Hack 2027* challenge, submissions are ranked primarily on **`spellF1`** (temporal IoU matching of maximal possession runs over thresholds $\tau \in \{0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7\}$), with **`macroF1`** serving as the tie-breaker. Under this evaluation paradigm, dead-ball (`D`) segmentation is the structural backbone of the entire possession pipeline:

1. **Protecting Possession Continuity (`spellF1`):** A false-positive `D` spike of even a few frames in the middle of a live possession splits a single true possession into two shorter spells, resulting in **one False Negative and two False Positives** under bipartite temporal IoU matching. Conversely, a brief false-negative drop inside a stoppage creates a phantom micro-possession.
2. **Boundary Precision ($\pm 1\text{ s}$ Penalty):** As demonstrated in `EVALUATION.md`, a 1-second (25-frame) boundary offset degrades `spellF1` from `1.0000` to `0.6539`. Because automated Smart Tagging exhibits a $\pm 2\text{--}5\text{ s}$ temporal jitter and player kinematics lag behind ball out-of-bounds events due to physical inertia, naive thresholding fails at the margins.
3. **Surviving 41% Missing Ball Frames:** The ball is absent on **40.7% of graded frames** (`94,214 / 158,857` visible). During set-piece setups and aerial restarts, the tactical detector frequently loses the ball (`ball_status_code > 0`).

To solve these challenges, we designed a deterministic, fully vectorized three-module architecture (`smart_tags.py`, `kinematic_d.py`, `d_model.py`) that fuses multi-camera tracking (Tactical + synchronized Broadcast), automated tactical priors, FIFA geometric exclusion laws, dual-edge impulse snapping, and a global 2-state Viterbi decoder in log-odds space.

## 4. Detailed Module Architecture & Implemented Rules

### 4.1. Module 1: Temporal Priors & `Team = N/A` Resolution (`src/deadball/smart_tags.py`)

1. **Frame Alignment & Jitter Buffering:** XML `<start>` and `<end>` timestamps (in seconds) are mapped to tactical video frames `[0..199318]` via $\text{frame} = \text{clip}(\text{round}(t_s \times 25), 0, 199318)$. Because SportsCode `SET PIECES` intervals include preparation and often extend $1\text{--}2\text{ s}$ past the restart kick, we construct a trapezoidal prior `sp_prior_prob` peaking at $0.88$ across $[f_{\text{start}} - 25, f_{\text{end}} - 25]$ with linear ramps over $\pm 3.5\text{ s}$ ($\pm 88$ frames).
2. **TV Replay Anchors (`brd_is_replay`):** When the broadcast director cuts away from the wide pitch view during active play (`brd_is_replay == 1`, `period > 0`), the game is almost certainly stopped. We project a dead-ball anchor shifted $35$ frames backward (accounting for the director's reaction time after the whistle) and truncate the final $30$ frames of the replay window (since TV feeds often return $1\text{--}2\text{ s}$ after the referee's restart whistle).
3. **Live-Play Suppression & Onset Signals:** Active offensive/transition tags (`ATTACKING TRANSITION`, `PROGRESSION`, `CREATING CHANCES`) outside set-piece windows generate a negative prior `live_play_suppression` (up to $0.35$) that prevents slow build-up phases from being misclassified as dead balls. Simultaneously, the start frame of `BUILD UP`, `LONG BALL`, and `PROGRESSION` tags generates a triangular `live_tag_onset_signal` ($\pm 12$ frames) that helps lock onto "Frame 0".
4. **Multi-Cue Resolution of `Team = N/A` (All 26 Throw-ins):** In the provided XML, 36 instances—including **100% of `Throw-in` tags (26/26)**—carry `Team = N/A`. Our solver `resolve_na_restart_team()` deterministically infers the restarting team (`H` vs `A`) by combining four vectorized cues around the restart window:
   * **Boundary Taker Geometry:** Weighting frames where `ball_near_boundary == 1` and one team has a player within $< 2.2\text{ m}$ of the ball while the opponent is $> 1.2\text{ m}$ further away (`away_near1_dist_m - home_near1_dist_m`).
   * **Exclusive 3m Control Zone:** Comparing the proportion of frames where exclusively one team occupies the $3\text{ m}$ control radius (`home_in_3m >= 1 & away_in_3m == 0` vs vice versa).
   * **Post-Restart Pitch Control Jump:** Integrating `pitch_ctrl_leadlag_25f` across $[f_{\text{end}} - 25, f_{\text{end}} + 35]$.
   * **Subsequent Tactical Tag Votes:** Summing team ownership votes from overlapping/subsequent live tags, automatically inverting polarity for defensive tags (`DEFENDING IN ...` tagged for Team $X$ votes for possession by Team $\neg X$).

### 4.2. Module 2: Vectorized Kinematic & Geometric Detector (`src/deadball/kinematic_d.py`)

This module supports both raw 46-slot ATD data (handling the `1..199319` to `0..199318` index shift and normalizing coordinates to a centered pitch $[-52.5, 52.5]\text{ m} \times [-34.0, 34.0]\text{ m}$) and the enriched `stage1_tracking.parquet` schema. It computes five core kinematic features plus three domain-specific indicators:

1. **Collective Player Speed (`mean_speed_all`):** Mean velocity (m/s) across all visible players.
2. **Walking Fraction (`walking_ratio_2ms`):** Fraction of visible players moving at walking pace ($< 2.0\text{ m/s}$), calibrated from `players_walking_ratio` ($< 1.5\text{ m/s}$) and collective speed.
3. **Inter-Team Separation / No Pressing (`no_pressing_dist_m`):** Computed either via chunked 3D tensor broadcasting `(chunk, N_H, N_A)` on raw slot coordinates or analytically from formation centroids and spatial variances:
   $$d_{\text{H-A}} = \sqrt{\|\boldsymbol{\mu}_H - \boldsymbol{\mu}_A\|_2^2 + \sigma_{H,x}^2 + \sigma_{A,x}^2 + \sigma_{H,y}^2 + \sigma_{A,y}^2}$$
4. **Multi-Camera Out-of-Bounds Detection (`ball_out_of_bounds`):** When the tactical ball is unobserved or interpolated (`ball_status_code > 0`) and the broadcast homography is valid (`brd_valid == 1`), we seamlessly substitute `brd_ball_x` and `brd_ball_y`. Coordinates are scaled from $[0, 105.3] \times [0, 68.0]$ to $[-52.5, 52.5] \times [-34.0, 34.0]$ to flag excursions beyond the pitch boundary, reinforced when the TV camera focal point (`brd_lookat_x/y`) parks on a touchline/goal line (`brd_lookat_speed_ms < 2.0`).
5. **Static Set-Piece Structures (`static_structure_flag`):**
   * *Free-Kick Wall (`free_kick_wall_flag`):* Stationary ball ($\ge 15$ frames), uncontested (`is_contested == 0`), high walking ratio ($\ge 0.65$), and $\ge 3$ defenders clustered in the $7.5\text{--}12.0\text{ m}$ ring with low lateral dispersion.
   * *Corner / Box Cluster (`corner_box_cluster_flag`):* $\ge 6$ players inside the penalty box (`players_in_penalty_box >= 6`) with a stationary ball and walking ratio $\ge 0.60$.
   * *Kick-Off & Goal-Kick Setups:* Ball at $(0, 0)$ with halves separated (`home_centroid_x_norm < 51.5` and `away_centroid_x_norm > 53.5`), or goalkeeper within $< 4.0\text{ m}$ of a stationary ball inside the goal area ($|x| \ge 45.0\text{ m}, |y| \le 11.0\text{ m}$).
6. **FIFA Opponent Exclusion Law (`opponent_exclusion_score`):** Distinguishes slow live build-up from set-piece preparation. During a dead ball, laws of the game enforce an exclusion zone around the ball ($2\text{ m}$ on throw-ins, $9.15\text{ m}$ on free kicks/corners/kick-offs, outside the penalty area on goal kicks). We score frames where the taker is within $< 3.0\text{ m}$ (`one_team_in3 == True`), the ball is stationary, and the nearest opponent respects a distance $> 3.5\text{--}9.0\text{ m}$.
7. **Possession State-Machine Void (`control_void_score` & Active Chain Suppression):** Uses `frames_since_last_control` rising above $25$ frames alongside high walking ratios to boost `D` probability, while actively suppressing `kinematic_d_score` by $60\%$ whenever `max(home_consecutive_touches, away_consecutive_touches) >= 2` and `frames_since_last_control <= 25` on an in-bounds ball.
8. **Ball Death Flux ($\Phi_{L \to D}$, `ball_death_flux`):** Captures the exact onset frame of a stoppage via the rising edge of `ball_out_of_bounds | ball_near_boundary` convolved with a 9-frame Hanning window, combined with sudden collective deceleration (`speed_drop`) immediately following a physical challenge (`is_contested == 1` within the preceding $35$ frames).

### 4.3. Module 3: Dual-Edge Snapping & Viterbi Decoding (`src/deadball/d_model.py`)

1. **Restart Impulse Signal ($\Phi_{D \to L}$, "Frame 0" Detector):**
   To pinpoint the exact frame where play resumes (`frame_zero`), `_compute_restart_impulse_signal()` fuses six physical and tactical edge detectors:
   * **Reliability-Weighted Ball Kick Impulse:** Combines ball acceleration (`ball_acc / 35.0`), 2-frame speed jump (`ball_speed - roll(ball_speed, 2)`), and trajectory deflection (`ball_deflection_angle`), gated by prior ball stationarity (`prev_stat`). Crucially, when the tactical ball is in a long tracking gap (`ball_status_code == 2`, reliability $0.15$), the model substitutes velocity and acceleration derived from synchronized broadcast ball coordinates (`brd_ball_x/y`, reliability $0.85$) or shifts weight to the taker's kinematics.
   * **Set-Piece Taker Burst:** Acceleration and closing speed (`near1_closing_speed_ms`) of the nearest player belonging to `restart_team_hint` within $3.2\text{ m}$ of the ball.
   * **State-Machine Contact Reset:** Detects the exact frame where `frames_since_last_control` drops from $\ge 30$ frames back to $\le 1$.
   * **Broadcast & Tactical Camera Whip-Pan:** Detects sudden camera acceleration (`brd_lookat_speed_ms` and `camera_pan_speed`) following long goal kicks, free kicks, and clearances (masked for the first $5$ frames after a broadcast replay cut using `frames_since_brd_replay > 5`).
   * **Lead/Lag Edge Filters:** Incorporates `|near1_diff_leadlag_10f|` and `|pitch_ctrl_leadlag_10f|`.
   * **Live Tactical Tag Onset:** Adds `live_tag_onset_signal` from `smart_tags.py`.

2. **Dual-Edge Boundary Snapping (`_snap_both_deadball_edges`):**
   * **Right Edge (`frame_zero`):** Inside each candidate set-piece window, the restart score is multiplied by a geometric compatibility factor `_compute_type_geometry_compatibility()` (verifying sideline proximity for `throw_in`, corner quadrant for `corner_kick`, goal area for `goal_kick`, and center circle for `kick_off`). At the argmax frame $t_0$, `p_dead` is boosted prior to $t_0$ and sharply decayed starting at $t_0$.
   * **Left Edge (`d_start`):** Eliminates the $1.5\text{--}2.5\text{ s}$ player deceleration lag by snapping the start of the dead-ball spell to the local maximum of `ball_death_flux` ($\Phi_{L \to D}$), suppressing false `D` probabilities on frames prior to $d_{\text{start}}$ when the ball was still travelling in bounds.
   * **Post-Replay Live Correction & Goalkeeper In-Play Proxy:** If the broadcast feed returns from a replay (`0 < frames_since_brd_replay <= 60`) and players are already running (`all_players_mean_speed > 2.55 m/s` or `ball_speed > 4.5 m/s`), `p_dead` is immediately multiplied by $0.18$. Similarly, when a goalkeeper holds the ball in live play (`gk_holding_ball_proxy != 0` outside set-piece windows), `p_dead` is suppressed by $0.22$ so live goalkeeper possession is not mistaken for a goal kick.

3. **Global 2-State Viterbi Decoder & Morphological Hysteresis (`_viterbi_and_posterior_decode`):**
   Rather than applying a local threshold, we transform `p_dead_snapped` into log-odds $L_t = \log\frac{p_t}{1 - p_t}$ and run dynamic programming (Viterbi algorithm) separately over each half (`period in (1, 2)`). Transition penalties between `LIVE (0)` and `DEAD (1)` are modulated dynamically by our edge fluxes:
   $$C_{L \to D}(t) = \max\big(1.5,\; \lambda_{\text{base}} - 8.5 \cdot \Phi_{L \to D}(t)\big), \qquad C_{D \to L}(t) = \max\big(1.5,\; \lambda_{\text{base}} - 9.5 \cdot \Phi_{D \to L}(t)\big)$$
   with $\lambda_{\text{base}} = 14.0$. Following Viterbi decoding, a two-stage morphological hysteresis filter bridges internal live gaps shorter than `min_live_gap_frames = 45` ($1.80\text{ s}$, e.g., a player tossing the ball to a teammate for a throw-in) and removes isolated dead-ball spikes shorter than `min_dead_duration_frames = 65` ($2.60\text{ s}$). Finally, `p_dead` is smoothly calibrated so that the threshold `p_dead >= 0.50` maps **1-to-1** to the globally optimal Viterbi state sequence ($[0.55, 0.99]$ for `DEAD`, $[0.01, 0.44]$ for `LIVE`), with hard domain overrides (`p_dead = 1.0`) for `period == 0`, `is_pre_kickoff_d == 1`, and `st_post_goal_dead == 1`.

## 5. Known Limitations & Future Improvements

1. **Quick Free Kicks in the Middle Third During Mutual Camera Occlusions:** When a quick free kick is taken in the center of the pitch within $< 2.5\text{ s}$ of a foul—while the broadcast camera is showing a close-up (`brd_is_replay == 1`), Smart Tagging misses the `Free Kick` tag, and the tactical ball is interpolated (`ball_status_code > 0`)—the morphological filter (`min_dead_duration_frames = 65`) may absorb the stoppage into the ongoing possession spell. *Future fix:* Integrate an optical-flow referee gesture / whistle audio-visual detector from the raw tactical MP4.
2. **Contested Drop-Balls and Referee Stoppages Without Set-Piece Tags:** Rare referee stoppages (e.g., head injury checks restarted via a drop-ball) do not carry a `SET PIECES` XML tag. While our `brd_is_replay` anchor and `opponent_exclusion_score` catch most of them, exact `frame_zero` detection is slightly less sharp than on sideline/corner restarts because there is no strict geometric boundary constraint.
3. **Throw-In Fake Swings:** Occasionally, a throw-in taker performs a fake throw motion while stepping along the touchline (`taker_acc` spikes while the ball remains in hands). Although `frames_since_last_control` and `ball_speed` prevent most premature triggers, when the ball is occluded (`ball_status_code == 2`), a fake run-up can shift `frame_zero` by $10\text{--}20$ frames early.

## 6. Output Schema & Reproduction Instructions

### 6.1. Output Schema (`data/interim/stage2_deadball.parquet`)

| Column | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `frame` | `int32` | Exact tactical video frame index `0 … 199318` in strictly ascending order. |
| `p_dead` | `float32` | Calibrated probability $[0.0, 1.0]$ that the ball is out of play (`D`). Threshold `p_dead >= 0.50` yields the exact Viterbi-optimal segmentation. Zero `NaN` values. |
| `is_set_piece_window` | `bool` | Active (`True`) inside expanded Smart Tagging set-piece anchors, post-goal intervals, and TV replay windows. |
| `restart_team_hint` | `category` (`'H'`, `'A'`, `NaN`) | Estimated team restarting play (`H` = FC Barcelona, `A` = Athletic Club), with all 26 `N/A` throw-ins and untagged stoppages resolved. |

### 6.2. How to Reproduce Using `uv`

The repository uses `uv` for deterministic dependency management. From the root directory of the repository:

```bash
# 1. Synchronize the virtual environment and pinned dependencies (numpy, pandas, pyarrow, kloppy)
uv sync

# 2. Run the production Stage 2 Dead-Ball pipeline (100% independent of any Ground Truth files)
uv run python -m src.deadball.d_model \
    --stage1 data/interim/stage1_tracking.parquet \
    --xml data/tactical/BAR-ATH_tactical_smart_tagging.xml \
    --output data/interim/stage2_deadball.parquet
```

*(Optional)* To run the read-only pipeline sanity check against the 5-minute public ground truth (`evaluation/gt/BAR-ATH_possession_gt_public.csv`) after generating the Parquet file:

```bash
uv run python -m src.deadball.d_model --check-public-gt
```
